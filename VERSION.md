## How to Check Your FFXI Version

### In-Game
1. Launch FFXI
2. Press **Esc** to open main menu
3. Go to **Config** → **Misc 2**
4. Check version number at bottom of screen

### File Check
Look for `FFXIROM.VER` file in your FFXI directory.
